These files are *derived* artifacts — feature matrices, key-step annotations, and
per-step baseline scores computed from agent rollouts on two existing benchmarks:

* **WebArena-Lite** — Liu et al., *VisualAgentBench*
* **Online-Mind2Web** — Xue et al., *An Illusion of Progress?*

Redistribution and reuse of anything derived from those benchmarks remains
subject to the terms of the original datasets. Check each project's own license
