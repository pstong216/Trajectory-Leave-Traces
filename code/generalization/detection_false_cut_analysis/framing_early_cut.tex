\subsection{Selective Prediction and Early-Cut Simulation}
\label{subsec:early_cut}

We next test whether the prefix-level onset score can support an early
stopping policy. A trajectory is cut once its score exceeds a threshold
before the final step. For failed trajectories, a cut counts as detected
only if it occurs at or after the annotated operational onset \(k^\star\);
successful trajectories contribute to False Cut Rate if they are cut at
all:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Det.} =
\frac{\#\{\text{failed trajs. cut at } k \in [k^\star, T)\}}
{\#\{\text{failed trajs.}\}},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{FCR} =
\frac{\#\{\text{successful trajs. cut at } k < T\}}
{\#\{\text{successful trajs.}\}}.
\end{equation}
We report Detection Rate under fixed false-cut budgets (\(5\%\)/\(10\%\)/\(20\%\))
and compare against Verbalized Confidence (M1), Action Entropy (M6), and
HTC Full (M11), split by benchmark.

Figure~\ref{fig:early_cut_tradeoff} and Table~\ref{tab:early_cut} show a
pattern that is stronger and more one-sided than the pooled prefix-time
result. On \textbf{Mind2Web}, Macro\&Micro is best at every budget we test
(\(32.2\%\), \(39.8\%\), \(49.3\%\) detection at \(5/10/20\%\) FCR),
\(9\)--\(20\) points ahead of HTC Full (\(12.7\%\), \(26.7\%\), \(40.4\%\))
throughout -- here HTC is the weakest of the three Ours variants rather
than the strongest baseline, unlike its early-region showing in the
prefix-time analysis (\S\ref{subsec:prefix_time}). On \textbf{WebArena-Lite},
the ordering among Ours' own feature sets is close: Macro-only slightly
leads at \(5\%\) FCR (\(34.6\%\) vs.\ Macro\&Micro's \(31.5\%\)), the two
are numerically tied at \(10\%\) FCR (\(42.0\%\) both), and Macro\&Micro
edges ahead again at \(20\%\) FCR (\(48.4\%\) vs.\ \(48.1\%\)); HTC Full
trails throughout this benchmark (\(25.6\%\), \(32.2\%\), \(44.1\%\)) but
narrows most of its gap by \(20\%\) FCR. Averaged across both benchmarks,
Macro\&Micro is best or tied-best at every budget we report: the
early-visibility advantage HTC Full showed in the prefix-time analysis
does not translate into a better early-cut policy under a tight false-cut
budget.
